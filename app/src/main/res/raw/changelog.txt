## 0.13.0
- Reminders: ⋮ → Remind me on any note. Pick later today, tomorrow morning, this weekend, next week or your own date and time, once or every day, week or month. The notification has Done and, for one-off reminders, In an hour. The bell chip shows on the note and its card, and the menu has a Reminders section, the next one first. They come back after the phone restarts.
- Share to Cenotes: share text, a link or pictures from any app and they become a new note.
- Quick Settings tile: add New note to the Quick Settings panel to start a note from anywhere.

## 0.12.1
- Updates: Install now opens Android's installer even when the download manager does not hand over the downloaded file (seen on the emulator, where Install did nothing).

## 0.12.0
- Trash: deleted notes go to the Trash (in the menu, under Archive) and are deleted for good after 7 days. From there you can restore them, delete them for good or empty the whole Trash.
- Note colour: give a note a soft background colour from its menu (⋮ → Colour), or to several at once from the selection.
- Checklists: ticked items move to the end and come back up when unticked (Settings → Notes), and you can drag items by their handle to put them in another order.
- Order of the notes: last edited, date created or title (Settings → Notes). Pinned notes always come first.

## 0.11.1
- The new note button is at the bottom right, round and 60 dp like Cemail's write button; search is a round button at the top.
- The bottom bar is smaller, with the same sizes as Cemail's bars.
- Retro 98: checkboxes look like Windows 98 ones (white, sunken edge, black tick).

## 0.11.0
- Cenotes now looks like Cemail: Settings is a tree of screens with big rows (Appearance, Label colours, Backup, Settings backup and Updates), with the same transitions.
- Appearance: Cemail's six designs (Glass, Modern, AMOLED, Retro 98, Paper and Brutalist), light or dark (or the system's), six background styles for the glass (Aurora, Classic, Neutral, Sunset, Ocean and Neon), 3D shadow and transparency.
- The eight colours of Celander and Cemail, plus a custom colour from a rainbow bar.
- Settings backup: save every setting to a file and restore it here or on another device.
- Updates: Cenotes looks for new versions on GitHub, shows them at the top of your notes, and Settings → Updates shows what each version brought.
- Cenotes in English and German as well as Spanish, following the phone's language.
- Its own switches and sliders, and buttons and cards that shrink when pressed and bounce back, as in Cemail.
