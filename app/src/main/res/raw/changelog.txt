## 2.0.1
- One single backup: the separate "Settings backup" page is gone. "Create backup" asks what to save (notes, images, settings), and Import restores any of them.

## 2.0.0
- Backups are now one button: "Create backup" asks, with check boxes, whether to save notes (with or without images) and/or settings in a single .zip.
- Cenotes is now signed with its own key. You need to reinstall it once: back up your notes and settings, uninstall, install this version and restore.

## 1.6.1
- A strip now sits behind the status bar and the top row, so notes no longer mix with the title and buttons when you scroll the list.
- Brutalist design: the hard shadow of the card returning to the list is now visible during the whole transition.

## 1.6.0
- Updates now download inside the app, with a progress bar and a Cancel button. If the connection drops, the download picks up where it left off.
- Selecting notes follows each design: its own border and tick in Brutalist, Retro 98, Paper and AMOLED.
- Every note now has its own identity, and Cenotes remembers the notes you delete for good. It is the first step towards syncing between devices. Your notes are updated by themselves the first time you open the app.
- Backups are now a .zip with your notes and your settings kept apart. When you import one, Cenotes shows what it holds and asks what to restore, with check boxes. Backups made by older versions can no longer be imported.
- Brutalist design: the hard shadow of a card is no longer cut off when you close a note.
- Behind the scenes: code tidy-up.

## 1.5.1
- Brutalist design: the hard shadow of note cards no longer gets cut off when you archive, delete or add a note, nor the one of the search bar, the link chip and the formatting bar as they appear.

## 1.5.0
- Checklists: an item with indented sub-items is now a dropdown. An arrow folds and unfolds it and, when folded, shows how many sub-items are done (for example 2/5). Drop an item on a folded one and it goes in as its last sub-item. Ticking the parent ticks all its sub-items.
- The list keeps its scroll position when you open a note and come back, and when you rotate the screen. Moving to another list starts at the top.

## 1.0.2
- Labels: opening a label now shows its archived notes too, as in Keep, and the side menu counts them. Archived notes carry a small archive icon next to their title.

## 1.0.1
- Backup: Import now lets you pick any file, so a backup .zip is no longer greyed out on devices that label it with another type. If the file is not a Cenotes backup, it says so. The same goes for importing settings.

## 1.0.0
- Cenotes 1.0: the first complete version. Notes, checklists and collapsible blocks; labels and note colours; reminders at a time or at a place; protected notes; history of each note; export, dictation and text from pictures; trash, archive and your own order; widgets, tablets and the six Ce designs, in English, Spanish and German.
- Retro 98: the new-note button has the same colour as the other buttons.

## 0.18.2
- TalkBack: when you select notes, it says how many are selected, not just the number.

## 0.18.1
- Settings: the cards keep their shadow on the sides too, instead of it being cut at the edge.

## 0.18.0
- The side menu looks like Cemail's: a floating card with Settings in its top corner, Notes, Reminders, Archive, Protected and Trash as buttons, and your labels below with their colour and count. Tap around it to close it.

## 0.17.2
- Buttons, Settings cards and dialogs show their 3D shadow while they appear, not only at the end.

## 0.17.1
- 3D shadow (Settings → Appearance) is now a soft shadow that lifts the glass off the background, the same as in Cemail.

## 0.17.0
- Reminders at a place: Remind me → At a place. Choose when you arrive or when you leave, then a place you saved, I'm here, or an address you look up. Places are saved with a name to use again. Cenotes asks once for location "all the time", so it can remind you while it is closed; your location stays on this device.
- Choosing a date and time is now a Cenotes card that follows your design, instead of Android's grey dialog.
- Tablets: the list and the open note share one background, without a seam between them.
- Texts speak of your device instead of your phone, and a repeating reminder shows its dot correctly.

## 0.16.0
- Order by hand: Settings → Notes → By hand. Hold a note and drag it where you want it; the others make room as you move it.
- History: ⋮ → History in a note shows how it was before each change (the last 20), and Restore brings any of them back. What was on screen is kept too.
- Tablets and open foldables: the list and the open note side by side, and Settings in a column you can move to either side, as in Cemail. Appearance → Note list puts the list on the left or right.
- Retro 98 and the other designs keep their own corners while a note opens and closes.
- TalkBack: switches and choices say whether they are on, colours have names, and notes and checklist items can be moved up and down without dragging.

## 0.15.0
- Collapsible blocks: the new icon in the formatting bar turns a line into a title with an arrow. What you write under it (Enter) goes inside, and tapping the arrow hides or shows it. Blocks can go inside one another, up to three deep; Enter on an empty line inside takes you out.
- The formatting bar also has icons for a code block and a dividing line, so every kind of line has its own button.

## 0.14.0
- Protected notes: ⋮ → Protect moves a note to Protected (in the menu). It disappears from your notes, search, widgets and shortcuts, and opens only with your phone's fingerprint, face or screen lock: one unlock opens all of them until you leave Cenotes or tap Lock. Their reminders say only "Protected note".
- Export: ⋮ → Export saves or sends a note as PDF (with its pictures), Markdown or plain text.
- Dictate: the microphone in the formatting bar writes what you say where the cursor is.
- Text from pictures: in a picture's menu, Copy text from picture reads the text on the phone and adds it under the picture.

## 0.13.0
- Reminders: ⋮ → Remind me on any note. Pick later today, tomorrow morning, this weekend, next week or your own date and time, once or every day, week or month. The notification has Done and, for one-off reminders, In an hour. The bell chip shows on the note and its card, and the menu has a Reminders section, the next one first. They come back after the phone restarts.
- Share to Cenotes: share text, a link or pictures from any app and they become a new note.
- Quick Settings tile: add New note to the Quick Settings panel to start a note from anywhere.

## 0.12.1
- Updates: Install now opens Android's installer even when the download manager does not hand over the downloaded file (seen on the emulator, where Install did nothing).

## 0.12.0
- Trash: deleted notes go to the Trash (in the menu, under Archive) and are deleted for good after 7 days. From there you can restore them, delete them for good or empty the whole Trash.
- Note colour: give a note a soft background colour from its menu (⋮ → Colour), or to several at once from the selection.
- Checklists: ticked items move to the end and come back up when unticked (Settings → Notes), and you can drag items by their handle to put them in another order.
- Order of the notes: last edited, date created or title (Settings → Notes). Pinned notes always come first.

## 0.11.1
- The new note button is at the bottom right, round and 60 dp like Cemail's write button; search is a round button at the top.
- The bottom bar is smaller, with the same sizes as Cemail's bars.
- Retro 98: checkboxes look like Windows 98 ones (white, sunken edge, black tick).

## 0.11.0
- Cenotes now looks like Cemail: Settings is a tree of screens with big rows (Appearance, Label colours, Backup, Settings backup and Updates), with the same transitions.
- Appearance: Cemail's six designs (Glass, Modern, AMOLED, Retro 98, Paper and Brutalist), light or dark (or the system's), six background styles for the glass (Aurora, Classic, Neutral, Sunset, Ocean and Neon), 3D shadow and transparency.
- The eight colours of Celander and Cemail, plus a custom colour from a rainbow bar.
- Settings backup: save every setting to a file and restore it here or on another device.
- Updates: Cenotes looks for new versions on GitHub, shows them at the top of your notes, and Settings → Updates shows what each version brought.
- Cenotes in English and German as well as Spanish, following the phone's language.
- Its own switches and sliders, and buttons and cards that shrink when pressed and bounce back, as in Cemail.
